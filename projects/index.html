Research

  • Quantization in diffusion vs autoregressive LMs

    Do masked diffusion LMs tolerate low-bit weights better than autoregressive models at matched scale? Matched pairs, thresholds set before each run, all on one 8 GB GPU. At INT4 the diffusion model degraded about half as much.

    2026 · whitepaper

  • Recurrent depth on a pretrained 4B model

    Seven attempts to retrofit a looped core onto FrogNano-4B, none better than a compute-matched LoRA. A training-free gain on HumanEval turned out to be reproduced by a noise control.

    2026 · negative result
